Hold the line until sunrise

HOPE: Survival is a top-down co-op survival game for 1–8 players — or play solo. Scavenge a quarantined island, craft what you need, and turn your base into the last warm light in a dying world.

The game

Every morning is a small victory

Noise is bait

Gunshots, explosions and the machines you run draw the infected in. Every shot is a decision — and a well-thrown stone can lure them away from where you need to be.

Some nights, they come

Not every night brings a horde. The ones that do hit harder than the last and grow with your squad. Make it to morning.

Build the last warm light

Turn a patch of ruins into a home: crafting stations, a furnace, extractors, garden beds, gates and traps. Keep upgrading it — it is the only safe place you have.

Survive together

Go it alone or bring up to seven friends. Split up to scavenge, regroup to defend — and when someone falls in the field, they rise again as one of them, carrying everything they had.

  • Real survivalHunger, thirst, fatigue, temperature, bleeding, broken bones — and food that spoils.
  • Close or rangedMelee combos, firearms, a bow, grenades and molotovs.
  • An island with a storyPiece together what happened here through notes scattered across the world.
  • Living weatherSnow, fog, rain and storms change how the island plays.

FAQ

Questions survivors ask

Can I play solo?

Yes. HOPE: Survival is fully playable alone, and the night hordes scale to the size of your group.

How many players can play together?

Up to 8 players in online co-op.

When does it come out, and how much will it cost?

The release date and price have not been announced yet. Wishlist the game on Steam and join our Discord to hear first.

Which platforms?

PC, on Steam.

Is there a story?

Yes. The island's story is told through notes you find scattered across the world.
